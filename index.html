How it works

TradeRef combines six plain-English factors into one player grade.

For trade comparisons, a player's trade value is their grade minus 45 (minimum 0), estimating the value above a readily available waiver replacement.

Rest-of-season projection

The analyst estimate of fantasy points per game from now through the end of the season.

This season's points

The player's current fantasy production per game, measured in half-PPR scoring.

Usage and touch share

Expected carries and targets per game, plus the percentage of the team's touches the player receives.

Last season's points

Previous-year fantasy points per game provide context for the player's experience and established role.

Injury status and missed weeks

Healthy, Questionable, Out or IR status is weighed alongside analysts' estimated weeks away.

Injuries to teammates

When teammates are unavailable, a player may get more targets or touches; the grade notes that expected role change.
